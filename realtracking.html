Got Questions?

Everything you need to know.

How does Apple AirTag work?
AirTag uses Bluetooth to communicate with nearby Apple devices in the Find My network, anonymously and securely relaying its location back to you. For close-range finding, Ultra Wideband technology provides precise directional guidance right to your item.
Is AirTag waterproof?
Yes. AirTag is rated IP67, which means it's dust-proof and can be submerged in water up to 1 meter deep for up to 30 minutes. It's built to handle rain, spills, and the occasional accidental plunge.
Does AirTag work worldwide?
Absolutely. The Find My network spans over a billion Apple devices across more than 80 countries. Whether your item is in Tokyo or Toronto, there's a strong chance it can be located.
How long does the battery last?
AirTag uses a standard CR2032 coin battery that lasts over a year with typical use. Your iPhone will notify you when the battery is running low, and replacing it takes just a twist — no tools required.
Which devices are compatible?
AirTag works with any iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch running iOS/iPadOS 14.5 or later. Precision Finding requires iPhone 11 or newer with Ultra Wideband support for the most accurate close-range tracking.
